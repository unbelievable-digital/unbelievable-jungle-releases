# Unbelievable Jungle — releases

Signed, notarized builds of **Unbelievable Jungle**, a desktop pet for macOS: a cat, a dog or a monkey that lives on top of your windows.

- **Download:** [UnbelievableJungle.zip](https://github.com/unbelievable-digital/unbelievable-jungle-releases/releases/latest/download/UnbelievableJungle.zip) — unzip and move to Applications. macOS 14 or later.
- **Updates:** installed copies update themselves through Sparkle, from `appcast.xml` in this repository.

## Coming from Unbelievable Cat?

Update Unbelievable Cat to 1.7.1 or later first (it offers the update on its own), then open Unbelievable Jungle. Your cat comes across with its name, how well it knows you, its office and its photos — and Unbelievable Cat closes itself once it has handed over.
